# Rice Meal
## Instructions

Mix 2 Tablespoons of Soy Sause + 1 tablespoon oyster sauce + 1/2 tbsp honey + 1 tbsp water + 2 clove minced galic (equ tbsp/tsp of bag) 1/2 tablespoon black pepper

slice 6 oz sliced beef (sirloin) and add to marinade - set aside (rest for 30 mins)

wash 1 cup of rice and add 1 cup of water
1/2 diced yellow onoins, 1/2 diced carrots, 1/2 cup diced pepper, 1/2 cup frozen corn

1 tbsp butter (optional)

Leave covered warm for 10
