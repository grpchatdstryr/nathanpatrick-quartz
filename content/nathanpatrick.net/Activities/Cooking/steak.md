# Steaks

## Instructions
*0.5-0.75 Inch Cut* 

**Part 1 - Preparation (30 - mins before)**  
*If Cooking by 12:00, get ready at 11:00*
1. Thaw and let the meat warm up (30-60 mins)
2. Prep materials

**Part 2 - Seasoning**  
2. Dry it (pat with paper towel)  
3. Oil the steak (not the grill, prevents sticking)  
4. Mix the seasoning ingredients together  
	- Kosher salt (1/4 teaspoon)
	- Black Pepper (1/4 teaspoon)
	- Canola Oil (enough to coat)
	- Liquid Smoke (like 8 total drops)
	- Rub the seasoning on steak
5. Let sit for 30 minutes

**Part 3 - Cooking**
1. Preheat grill (leave plugged in for 5 mins, or until the light turns green)
2. Place steak on and close (don't walk away)
3. Cook for:
	- Medium-Rare (Pink center): Check after 2 minutes 30 seconds (check)
	- Medium (Light pink): Check after 3 minutes (might take 4 depending on thickness)
4. Check Thermometer
	- 125°F (54°C) - 135°F (60°C): Pull it off now for Medium.

**Part 4 - Finishing**
1. Immediately after taking it off the grill, put it on a plate
2. Place slab of (salted?) butter on top
3. Place aluminum foil loosely over the plate, wait 5(to 10) minutes
4. Enjoy
