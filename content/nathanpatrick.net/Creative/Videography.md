# Videography
Have edited multiple music videos for friends and for fun. Currently use DaVinci Resolve, but have used Premier Pro in the past. Mainly shoot video from my phone.


## Music Video for a Friend.
All I did was the videography and scene planning. I had no influence on the lyrics and ideas, but had fun filming and editing the entire video. This was reviewed by YouTube Channel with over 3 Million Subscribers which their review video has over 300,000 views. This video is my friend's channel's most watched music video of all his music video content.
